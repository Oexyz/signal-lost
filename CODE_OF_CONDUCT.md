# Code of conduct

SIGNAL//LOST contributors are expected to make technical collaboration safe, focused, and respectful.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss ideas and code without attacking people.
- Give specific, actionable review feedback.
- Respect privacy, spoiler boundaries, and responsible security reporting.
- Welcome contributors with different backgrounds and levels of experience.
- Accept correction and help repair harm when a discussion goes wrong.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, stalking, or discriminatory conduct.
- Publishing another person's private information without permission.
- Sexualized attention or imagery in project spaces.
- Deliberately disrupting collaboration or security-reporting channels.
- Retaliation against a person who raises a good-faith concern.

## Scope and enforcement

This policy applies in repository spaces and when representing the project elsewhere. Report conduct concerns privately to `security@oexyz.de` with the subject `SIGNAL//LOST conduct report`.

Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary to protect the community. Enforcement decisions should be proportionate, documented privately where appropriate, and avoid exposing sensitive information.
