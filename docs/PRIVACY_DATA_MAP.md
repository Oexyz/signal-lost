| Data category | Purpose | Source | Storage | Retention | Access | Recipients | Deletion | Status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

## Browser storage inventory

No cookies, local storage, session storage, IndexedDB, Cache API, or service worker are used by the Phase 0 browser application.

## Planned but not implemented

Temporary nicknames, random room identifiers, resume credentials, invite-only room state, game events, private choices, and Evidence Board text require a map update before implementation.
